FAQ

Common questions

What does “5-year ownership cost” mean?

It is an estimate of the money consumed by owning the car for five years, including financing and running costs, minus the estimated resale value at the end of year five.

Why is monthly cash commitment different from true monthly ownership cost?

Cash commitment shows what leaves your budget each month right now. True monthly ownership cost spreads the estimated five-year economic cost across 60 months.

Can I compare an EV with a petrol or diesel car?

Yes. Select EV for one car and petrol, diesel or hybrid for the other. EV mode uses electricity consumption and electricity price.

Are the results exact?

No. They are planning estimates. Real insurance, repairs, taxes, energy use, loan terms and resale prices vary by driver, vehicle and location.

How CarCost calculates it

Monthly cash commitment is the loan payment plus recurring running costs. Five-year ownership cost counts the down payment, loan payments made in the first 60 months, any remaining loan balance at month 60, and five years of running costs, then subtracts the estimated resale value. This avoids counting depreciation on top of the full purchase price.
