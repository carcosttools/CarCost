PRIVACY

Privacy by design.

CarCost does not require an account, password, payment card or banking information. Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not stored in a CarCost database.

METHODOLOGY

Transparent methodology.

CarCost uses the information you provide to estimate financing, operating costs and resale value. Actual ownership costs vary by vehicle, driver, market and location.

FAQ

Is CarCost financial advice?

No. It is a planning calculator designed to make costs easier to understand.

Do you store calculator data?

No CarCost database stores the values entered into the calculator.

Is the first prototype always free?

No. It is offered only for a limited number of suitable early projects.
