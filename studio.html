ABOUT CARCOST

Focused digital tools. Clear outcomes.

CarCost was built around a simple principle: useful digital tools should be clear, reliable and easy to use.

01

Purpose before complexity

We begin with the core user problem and avoid features that do not improve the outcome.

02

Clarity by design

Interfaces should make the result easy to understand without adding unnecessary cognitive load.

03

Trust through transparency

We communicate clearly about limitations, data handling and what the product can realistically deliver.

PRIVACY

Privacy by design.

CarCost does not require an account, password, payment card or banking information. Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not stored in a CarCost database.

METHODOLOGY

Transparent methodology.

CarCost uses the information you provide to estimate financing, operating costs and resale value. Actual ownership costs vary by vehicle, driver, market and location.

FAQ

Is CarCost financial advice?

No. It is a planning calculator designed to make costs easier to understand.

Do you store calculator data?

No CarCost database stores the values entered into the calculator.

Are the estimates guaranteed?

No. Results are planning estimates and actual ownership costs can differ.
