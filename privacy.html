CarCost

PRIVACY

Private by design.

CarCost does not require an account, password, payment card or banking information.

Your calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not stored in a CarCost database.

CarCost is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process technical data required to serve the website under its own policies.

The project contact form does not silently store submissions. It creates a request that you can copy and send through the contact page.
