ABOUT

CarCost is a free car ownership comparison tool.

It helps people compare two cars using financing, fuel or electricity, insurance, maintenance, taxes, parking and estimated resale value.

Why it exists

A purchase price alone does not show the real cost of owning a car. CarCost brings the main cost categories into one comparison so users can make a more informed estimate.

How results are calculated

CarCost uses the values you enter. Monthly cash commitment includes loan payments and recurring running costs. Five-year ownership cost includes the down payment, loan payments made during the first 60 months, any remaining loan balance at month 60, five years of running costs, and then subtracts the estimated resale value.

Important limitation

CarCost provides planning estimates only. Real fuel use, electricity use, repairs, insurance, tax, finance rates and resale values vary. CarCost is not financial advice.
